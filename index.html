/ 05 — ABOUT DEMUFM

Built to understand.

DEmuFM is an independent arcade emulation project developed with Delphi and FireMonkey.

AN INDEPENDENT
ARCADE EMULATION JOURNEY

Emulation is more than running a game.

It's about understanding the systems behind it: CPUs, memory maps, graphics, audio chips, timing and the tiny details that make classic software feel authentic.

DEmuFM is developed with Delphi and FireMonkey, alongside SDL. For now, its public-facing work is focused exclusively on Arcade emulation.
